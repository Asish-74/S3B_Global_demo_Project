DAUER CLASSIC CARS

A hobby that became a homage to the past.

The brainchild of Eddie and Joanne Dauer, Dauer Classic Cars is marked by their passion for and appreciation of our nation's rich history.

What started as a hobby long ago transformed into an incredible homage to the past. The couple's love of antique cars became a driving force in their lives for nearly 50 years.

As the collection grew, so did the realization that these automobiles and the stories surrounding them deserved to be shared.

The story behind Dauer Classic Cars

THE BEGINNING

A shared love of automobiles.

From his college years, Eddie was drawn to collecting cars. He even picked up Joanne in a 1941 Cadillac sedan for their very first date. After years of work restoring cars, they sold it and purchased a 1941 Cadillac limousine — their first car together as a couple.

Since then, they have saved every single car they have purchased and created a collection that continued to grow. The Dauers soon realized they had quite a treasure on their hands, and an outstanding treasure deserved to be shared.

Officially opening its doors in 2001, Dauer Classic Cars became a place where South Florida residents and visitors could step away from the present and spend time surrounded by the design, technology and culture of earlier generations.
